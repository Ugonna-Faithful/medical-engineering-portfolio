# Clinical Engineering

Work at the interface of engineering and the healthcare setting: medical
equipment lifecycle management, device governance and risk, clinical workflow,
and the operational context in which medical devices are deployed and used.

## Projects

### [Critical Analysis of a Medical Devices Management Policy](medical-devices-policy-analysis/)
A lifecycle-based critical analysis of a published NHS Trust medical devices
management policy, assessing its governance across the seven-phase equipment
lifecycle, scoring the key gaps with an ISO 14971 risk analysis, and specifying
the KPI needed to close each one.
**Skills:** device lifecycle management · ISO 14971 risk assessment · regulatory and standards analysis · governance and KPI design
