# Quality Control & Regulatory Affairs

Work in medical-device standards, risk management, and multi-market regulatory
compliance — mapping requirements such as ISO 13485, ISO 14971, and IEC 60601-1,
and building the risk and quality documentation that underpins device approval.

*Projects in this discipline are being added. A cross-jurisdictional device
compliance framework (mapping ISO 14971 and IEC 60601-1 across six regulatory
systems) is in preparation.*
