# Quality Control & Regulatory Affairs

Work in medical-device standards, design controls, risk management, and
multi-market regulatory compliance: mapping requirements such as ISO 13485, ISO
14971, IEC 60601-1, and FDA 21 CFR 820, and building the design and quality
documentation that underpins device development and approval.

## Projects

### [Design Control Methodologies for a Medical Device Translation Guidance Resource](design-control-methodologies/)
A critical analysis of the design control process behind a guidance resource
that walks academic researchers through the full medical device translation
pathway, from concept through regulatory approval, clinical validation, patient
access, and disposal. Produced within a cross-functional team I coordinated as
lead; covers the Statement of Need, design specification (XDS), and structured
weighted concept selection under ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR 820, and EU/UK MDR
frameworks.
**Skills:** team leadership, design control methodology, design documentation, requirements traceability, concept selection
