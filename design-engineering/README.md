# Design Engineering

Mechanical and electronic design work: parametric CAD, mechanical testing and
characterisation, packaging, and design for manufacture, from component modelling
and analysis through to engineering drawings and formed packaging.

## Projects

### [Bone Plate Modelling: Volar Distal Radius Fixation Plate](bone-plate-modelling/)
A parametric FreeCAD model of a volar distal radius fixation plate (an orthopaedic
implant for wrist fractures), with full orthographic engineering drawings,
dimensioned features, a section view, and a parametric Python macro that generates
the geometry.
**Skills:** parametric 3D CAD, engineering drawing, orthopaedic device geometry, material and standards awareness

### [Packaging Design: Vacuum-Formed Instrument Trays](packaging/)
Vacuum-formed (thermoformed) trays designed to hold precision instruments in
moulded cavities, covering cavity and draft design for the forming process.
Packaging design only; the instruments are existing tools.
**Skills:** thermoform packaging design, cavity and draft design, design for manufacture

### [Mechanical Testing of Cancellous Bone](cancellous-bone-testing/)
Uniaxial compression testing of porcine and bovine cancellous bone, with a
density-corrected statistical comparison of stiffness, strength, and failure
strain, showing that density explains stiffness but not the higher strength of
bovine bone.
**Skills:** mechanical testing, stress-strain analysis, property-density regression, statistical comparison
