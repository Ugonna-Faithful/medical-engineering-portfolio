# Research & Development

Investigative and experimental work: signal processing, algorithm development,
designed experiments, instrumentation, and optimisation. These projects show the
ability to take a problem from raw data or first principles through to a
validated, interpreted result.

## Projects

### [Surface EMG Signal Processing and Analysis](biosignal-emg/)
A complete MATLAB biosignal pipeline: normalisation, Butterworth filtering,
feature extraction, muscle identification, and quantitative fatigue analysis of
surface electromyography.
**Skills:** biosignal processing, digital filter design, FFT, feature extraction, MATLAB

### [Factorial Design of Experiment: Paper Helicopter Optimisation](factorial-doe/)
A 2^4 full factorial experiment with ANOVA, interaction and curvature analysis,
response optimisation, and a sensitivity study, finding the design that maximises
flight time and interpreting the result through aerodynamic mechanism.
**Skills:** experimental design, analysis of variance, regression modelling, statistical optimisation

### [Electrooculography: Eye Movement Recording and Analysis](eog-eye-movements/)
Recording and analysis of eye movements using surface electrodes: electrode
setup, angular calibration (R squared = 0.99), artefact identification, and
measurement of saccades, smooth pursuit, and gaze-holding reflexes.
**Skills:** biosignal acquisition, sensor calibration, artefact characterisation, measurement-standards awareness

### [Statistical Analysis of an Ingestible Drug Delivery Robot](drug-delivery-robot-doe/)
A 3x2 two-way factorial study of how orifice diameter and vacuum volume affect a
drug delivery robot's attachment time, analysed with two-way ANOVA, partial
eta-squared effect sizes, assumption checks, and Tukey HSD post-hoc testing.
**Skills:** two-way ANOVA, effect-size reporting, assumption testing, post-hoc analysis
