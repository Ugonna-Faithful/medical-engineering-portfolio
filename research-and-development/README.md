# Research & Development

Investigative and experimental work: signal processing, algorithm development,
designed experiments, and optimisation. These projects show the ability to take
a problem from raw data or first principles through to a validated, interpreted
result.

## Projects

### [Surface EMG Signal Processing and Analysis](biosignal-emg/)
A complete MATLAB biosignal pipeline — normalisation, Butterworth filtering,
feature extraction, muscle identification, and quantitative fatigue analysis of
surface electromyography.
**Skills:** biosignal processing · digital filter design · FFT · feature extraction · MATLAB

### [Factorial Design of Experiment — Paper Helicopter Optimisation](factorial-doe/)
A 2⁴ full factorial experiment with ANOVA, interaction and curvature analysis,
response optimisation, and a sensitivity study — finding the design that
maximises flight time and interpreting the result through aerodynamic mechanism.
**Skills:** experimental design · analysis of variance · regression modelling · statistical optimisation
